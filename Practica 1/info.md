# Dataset.csv
## Descripcion
Conjunto de datos de 140 objetos pertenecientes al videojuego Guild Wars 2, rastreados a diario para analizar su comportamiento en el mercado del videojuego a lo largo de 5 años. (2021/08/29 - 2026/08/29)

## Variables
count: The number of raw data snapshots taken during this 24-hour period to calculate these metrics.
date: The specific day the data was recorded.
itemID: The unique identification number of the asset or item being tracked.
type: The type of object the item is assigned in-game.

buy_delisted: Total number of buy orders canceled or removed by players without being filled.
buy_listed: Total number of buy orders created or posted to the market during the day.
buy_sold: Total number of items actually bought by players filling these orders (completed transactions).
buy_value: The total currency volume generated by the completed buy transactions.
buy_price_avg: The average unit price offered across all active buy orders.
buy_price_max / buy_price_min: The highest and lowest unit prices offered in the buy order book.
buy_price_stdev: Standard deviation of the buy price.
buy_quantity_avg: The average number of items requested per buy order.
buy_quantity_max / buy_quantity_min: The largest and smallest single order sizes requested.
buy_quantity_stdev: Standard deviation of order sizes; shows if order volumes fluctuate wildly.

sell_delisted: Total number of sell orders canceled or removed by players without being filled.
sell_listed: Total number of sell orders posted to the market during the day.
sell_sold: Total number of items actually sold by players filling these orders (completed transactions).
sell_value: The total currency volume generated by the completed sell transactions.
sell_price_avg: The average unit price requested across all active sell orders.
sell_price_max / sell_price_min: The highest and lowest unit prices requested in the sell order book.
sell_price_stdev: Standard deviation of the sell price.
sell_quantity_avg: The average number of items offered per sell order.
sell_quantity_max / sell_quantity_min: The largest and smallest single supply batches posted.
sell_quantity_stdev: Standard deviation of sell order sizes.

name: The name as stored in-game.
description: A brief description of the item as stored in-game.
rarity: An assigned characteristic tied to the itemID of an object in-game.
level: The minimum level required to use the item in-game.
vendor_value: The unit price at which NPC vendors buy the item in-game.
flags: Restrictions put upon the item in-game.

## Fuentes
Para la obtencion de este conjunto de datos, por medio de un script se trajeron datos de las 2 siguientes APIs:
### Endpoint del historico por objeto
https://api.datawars2.ie/gw2/v2/history/csv?itemID=<id>
### Endpoint de la metadata del objeto
https://api.guildwars2.com/v2/items?ids=<id>&lang=en
